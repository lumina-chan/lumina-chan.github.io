what i'm up to

01: building things

i started with the basics of Python and kept asking questions.

that curiosity took me through CS50P, regular expressions, APIs, file handling, testing, object-oriented programming, and a bunch of small experiments that eventually became real projects.

some things i've built or explored:

  • Kumakun: a Python CLI for detecting, validating, and parsing structured information.
  • PIPPO: a little Discord bot with ideas for a much bigger future.
  • Student Database: a project that taught me to think beyond making code work once.
  • Binary Friendship Terminal: because apparently even friendship can become a programming experiment.
  • Lumina OS: an evolving idea of a personal digital assistant and a space for my own tools.

i like figuring things out myself. i'd rather understand why something works than blindly copy something impressive.

and yes, sometimes the code breaks. sometimes the idea changes halfway through. sometimes i start three things before finishing one.

we're working on that last part.

things i believe in

  • curiosity over pretending. it's okay not to know. it's better to ask.
  • understanding over appearances. i'd rather build something meaningful than look like i'm building something meaningful.
  • experimentation over endless planning. at some point, you have to try the thing.
  • documentation over forgetting. ideas, mistakes, and small victories deserve to leave a trail.
  • independence over imitation. inspiration is good. having your own mind matters more.
  • kindness with boundaries. being a good person doesn't mean being available for everything.
  • progress without pretending to be finished. unfinished doesn't mean incapable. it means there's more to do.
